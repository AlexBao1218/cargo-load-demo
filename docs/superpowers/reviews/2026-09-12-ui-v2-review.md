# UI v2 design review — cargo-load-demo

Date: 2026-09-12
Reviewed: `http://localhost:4190` at 1280×800 and 375×812 (Chrome), plus `docs/screenshots/desktop-v2.png` / `mobile-v2.png`.
Exercised: load, card scroll (wheel + tabs), all three scenarios, drag chip→tile, tap-to-place, double-click lock, panel Lock/Unload, Optimize, Reset, invalid-drop toast, drawer, mobile bottom strip.
Measurements below are DOM/computed-style values at 1280×800 unless marked "mobile".

Overall: v2 is structurally right (vertical aircraft, bordered scroll card, three zones) and is a big step up from v1. What still stops it from reading as "warm, restrained, refined" is (a) the aircraft itself, which is faint and clipped at the wrong edge so it looks like a bug, (b) a CG gauge whose target band is literally invisible, (c) scroll-snap hijacking the wheel, and (d) a layer of generic SaaS ornament (grip handles, bordered mini-pills, lightning-bolt icon, red/amber score badges, 34 simultaneous focus rings) that reads as template output. Everything below is ordered by priority; the P0s are the ones the owner will notice in the first ten seconds.

---

## P0 — must fix before the owner sees it

### 1. Wings and tailplanes are clipped by the SVG viewBox, not by the card — they end mid-card with a hard vertical edge
**Saw:** At 1280 the card body is 638 px wide and the canvas is 480 px, centred, so there is 79 px of white card on each side of the canvas. The wing paths (`WING_REACH = 260`) and stabilisers (`STAB_REACH = 300`) run past x = 480 / x < 0 and get cut by the `<svg viewBox="0 0 480 1400">` at the canvas edge — a hard vertical cut at viewport x ≈ 232 and x ≈ 712, with plain white card to the left/right of the cut. Same for the stabilisers at the tail. The addendum promised "clipped by the card"; what ships looks like a rendering error ("why does the wing stop in the middle of nothing?"). On mobile (card 349 px, canvas 480 px) the card does the clipping and it looks correct — which proves the intent.
**Why:** It's the single most visible defect on first load and directly undermines "accurate 747-8F silhouette".
**Fix:** `src/components/Fuselage.tsx`: add `overflow="visible"` to the `<svg>` (and `style={{ overflow: "visible" }}` to be safe), so the scroll container's `overflow-x-hidden` (already on the scroller in `AircraftCard.tsx`) does the clipping at the card edge. Extend `WING_REACH` to ≥ 360 and `STAB_REACH` to ≥ 360 so the wing/stab always reach the card edge at every width ≥ 768. Alternatively give the outer wrapper `w-full` and compute the SVG width from `clientWidth` — but `overflow: visible` is the two-line fix.

### 2. The CG gauge's target band is invisible (1.02 : 1 against the track)
**Saw:** `CgGauge.tsx` draws the track as `bg-line/50` (≈ #eceef2 on white) and the ±2 m band as `bg-jade-soft` (jade @ 12 % ≈ #e2eeed). Measured contrast between the two: **1.02 : 1**. The band is also only `(4 / 57.3) × 256 px ≈ 18 px` wide because the scale spans the full arm range 7.7–65 m. What the user actually sees is a grey line, a 1-px hairline and a needle; the "am I inside tolerance" question the gauge exists to answer is not answerable from it.
**Why:** The gauge is the hero metric of the whole demo and the sticky strip's reason to exist.
**Fix:** `src/components/CgGauge.tsx`:
- Band: `bg-jade/20` (or a 1.5 px jade outline segment) and increase track height to `h-2`.
- Narrow the domain: pass `min = target − 8`, `max = target + 8` from `CgStrip.tsx` (clamp the needle, and when the CG is outside the window show a small "◂ 7.7 m" caret at the edge). At ±8 m the band becomes 64 px of 256.
- Add the two band-edge values as 10 px `tabular` tick labels under the track (`34.0 · 38.0`) instead of the meaningless NOSE / TAIL words — the nose/tail direction is already established by the aircraft above.

### 3. Scroll-snap hijacks the wheel and trackpad inside the aircraft card
**Saw:** `AircraftCard.tsx` sets `scrollSnapType: "y proximity"` with `scrollSnapAlign: "start"` on each section's first tile plus anchors at both ends. Measured: `scrollBy(150)` from the top lands at **297 px**; `scrollBy(250)` also lands at **297 px**; three wheel ticks (300 px) from 201 landed at 597 then settled to 592. Any scroll that ends within ~150 px of a section start gets yanked to it; the active tab label also flashes the wrong section for ~200 ms while the snap settles.
**Why:** With only 4 snap points in 1400 px the behaviour is inconsistent — sometimes the list scrolls freely, sometimes it jumps — which is exactly the "unexplained" feeling the owner complained about in v1. Seat maps do not snap.
**Fix:** Remove `scrollSnapType` and both anchor divs and the per-tile `scrollSnapAlign`/`scrollMarginTop` in `AircraftCard.tsx`. Keep tab-click smooth scrolling in `SectionTabs.tsx`; that already gives "go to section".

### 4. The aircraft is too faint to read as an aircraft; the tiles are the only thing with any weight
**Saw:** Hull fill `#fbfbfc` on a `#ffffff` card (1.03 : 1), stroke `#c9ced6` @ 1.5 px (1.58 : 1), wings/nacelles/stabs additionally at `opacity 0.7`, L/R labels at `opacity 0.55` (2.31 : 1). At arm's length the silhouette disappears and the page reads as two columns of green rectangles floating on white. The hump outline and the cockpit arc (a 60 px arc at y 74–86) are practically invisible.
**Why:** The whole point of v2 was that the drawing "reads as a 747 at a glance". Right now it reads as "some rounded shape".
**Fix:** `src/index.css` / `Fuselage.tsx`: set `--color-fuselage: #f3f4f6` (one step below the page `#f5f6f7`, so the hull is a plate darker than the card), hull stroke `#b8bec7` at 1.5 px, wings/stabs at full opacity with stroke `#c9ced6` and fill `#f8f9fa`, remove the extra `opacity={0.7}` group. Hump: stroke `#b8bec7` 1 px, fill `#eef0f3` so the upper deck reads as a raised region rather than a pencil line. Drop the `L`/`R` letters to `opacity 0.8`.

## P1 — should fix

### 5. Engine nacelles look like pods glued to the fuselage
**Saw:** `NACELLE_OFFSETS = [28, 70]` px outboard of the body side, 24×54 rounded rects, drawn ahead of the leading edge. Because the canvas clips at 90 px outboard, both nacelles sit right beside the hull (viewport x ≈ 250 and 292 on the left) and read as two thumb-shaped blobs at the wing root — nothing like a 747's engines, which sit at ~40 % and ~70 % of semi-span.
**Fix:** Once (1) is done and the wings run to the card edge, move the offsets to `[140, 260]`; on narrower cards they'll be clipped, which is fine. If the wings stay short, delete the nacelles entirely — a wing root without engines reads better than a wing root with two blisters.

### 6. 110 px of empty nose above the first tile on every load
**Saw:** Scroller top at y = 171; A1 tile top at y = 281 (`NOSE_Y = 40`, `Y_MIN = 110`). On mobile the same 110 px is 21 % of the 512 px card body. First paint therefore shows nose + 3 nose tiles + 4 main-deck rows; the remaining 11 rows and R1 are below the fold.
**Fix:** `src/components/layout.ts`: `Y_MIN = 78`; `Fuselage.tsx`: `NOSE_Y = 16`, shift `NOSE_FULL_Y`, `HUMP_END_Y`, cockpit and wing/stab constants up by the same 32 px (or better: derive them from `NOSE_Y`). Gains one more visible row on both breakpoints.

### 7. After Optimize every filled tile gets a 2 px jade ring for 1.6 s — 34 rings at once
**Saw:** `PositionTile.tsx` applies `ring-2 ring-jade ring-offset-1` for `recentlyChanged`. Jade ring on a jade tile with a 1 px white gap = a double border on every tile simultaneously; combined with the `tileIn` stagger it looks like a selection bug rather than a highlight.
**Fix:** Drop the ring for `recentlyChanged`; the `tileIn` scale/opacity stagger is enough. If you want a settle cue, animate the tile background from `jade-deep` to `jade` over 600 ms (`@keyframes settle { from { background-color: var(--color-jade-deep) } }`).

### 8. Score is a filled red badge the moment the first ULD is placed
**Saw:** Place one ULD in A1 → CG 7.7 m, strip shows `Score 0` in `bg-danger` white text, needle red at the far-left end of the track (outside the rounded cap). The first thing the demo says to a new user is "0, red". Also amber `#b7791f` with white 11 px text is **3.64 : 1** (fails AA).
**Fix:** `CgStrip.tsx`: render the score as text, not a filled pill — `<span class="tabular text-xs">Score <b class="font-semibold">66</b></span>` preceded by a 6 px dot coloured jade / amber / danger. Treat the score as provisional while `loadedCount < flight.ulds.length`: show it in muted ink with a "partial" dot (line-soft) and only colour it once every ULD is on board. Same colour logic for the needle.

### 9. Empty-tile text fails contrast and is redundant on 31 of 34 tiles
**Saw:** Empty tile second row is 10 px `text-muted/80` ≈ #7c838c on white → **3.83 : 1**. Every main-deck tile says `AKE/AMA ≤4,820`; only A1/A2/B1 differ (`AKE`). The `≤` glyph reads as maths, not as "max".
**Fix:** `PositionTile.tsx`: second row for empty tiles = `<span class="tabular text-[11px] text-muted">4,820 kg</span>` only; show a type note only where it is an exception: `AKE only` on nose tiles. Raise the text to `text-muted` (6 : 1). The capacity bar track on empty tiles (`bg-line/60`, 3 px) can go — an empty bar says nothing.

### 10. Locked badge is a black dot; the hover-reveal lock floats outside the tile
**Saw:** Lock button is a 24 px circle at `-top-1.5 -right-1.5`; locked = `bg-ink` (#1c1f23) — the only near-black fill on the page, so it reads as a notification badge. Unlocked hover reveal is a white circle with a grey open-lock, partially overlapping the tile above's bottom edge.
**Fix:** Move the lock inline: in the tile header row, `flex justify-between` with the id on the left and a 10 px `Lock` glyph on the right (`text-white/80` when locked, invisible until `group-hover` when not). Locked tile additionally gets `border-jade-deep bg-jade-deep`. Delete the absolutely positioned button; keep it as a `sr-only`-styled but focusable control if you need keyboard access, or rely on the panel's Lock/Unlock.

### 11. Panel hint row wraps to three lines at 11 px next to two buttons
**Saw:** Select a placed ULD → the panel inserts a grey row: `ULD-S in DL · tap a highlighted position to move it` at 11 px in ~135 px of width (3 lines) with `Lock` and `Unload` buttons on the right. It also pushes the list down 24–48 px (layout shift).
**Fix:** `UldPanel.tsx`: put the hint as one line in the header (`Unassigned` → `ULD-S · DL`), and the two actions as icon-only 32 px buttons on the header's right. Or reserve a fixed 40 px "context" row that is always present (empty when nothing is selected) so nothing shifts.

### 12. Generic-SaaS ornament: grip handles, bordered type pills, lightning bolt
**Saw:** Every panel row starts with `GripVertical` (34 grips in a column); every row and every mobile chip has a bordered 10 px uppercase `AKE`/`AMA` pill; Optimize uses the lucide `Zap` bolt. Together with the coloured score badge these are the tells that make it look like a generated dashboard.
**Fix:** `UldChip.tsx`: remove `GripVertical` (rows already have `cursor-grab` and a hover bg; on mobile chips the whole chip is the handle). Replace `TypePill` with plain `<span class="text-[11px] text-muted">AKE</span>`. `CgStrip.tsx`: Optimize with no icon (or a 13 px `Sparkles`-free glyph such as `ArrowRightLeft`); keep the spinner while solving. `TopBar.tsx`: drop the `BookOpen` icon on "How it works" — text buttons look calmer.

### 13. Two 57 px white toolbars stacked = 114 px of chrome before the content (mobile: 182 px)
**Saw:** `header` 0–57 and the CG strip 57–114, both `bg-surface border-b`, both `h-14`. Mobile: header 99 px (title row + full-width segmented) + strip 83 px. The eye hits three horizontal borders before the aircraft.
**Fix:** `TopBar.tsx` → `md:h-12`, no `border-b`; `CgStrip.tsx` → `md:h-12`, keep its `border-b`; export `STRIP_H = 48` and adjust `md:h-[calc(100dvh-214px)]` (AircraftCard) and `h-[calc(100dvh-172px)]` (UldPanel) by −16. On mobile, fold the segmented control into the strip's second row (it is a scenario, i.e. data, not chrome) and let the title row be 44 px.

### 14. Tab activity lags and mislabels at the tail
**Saw:** Scrolling to the end sets the active tab to `Tail` even though 10 aft tiles and one tail tile are visible; during a tab-click scroll the underline moves instantly while `font-medium` on the previous tab lingers a frame. Aft is effectively never "active" at the bottom of the card.
**Fix:** `SectionTabs.tsx`: remove the `atEnd → Tail` clamp; use the section with the most visible tile area instead of the one nearest the centre. Apply weight and underline from the same state (they are — the lingering is the 150 ms `transition-colors`; drop the transition on font-weight-bearing text).

### 15. Toast overlaps the bottom edge of the aircraft card
**Saw:** `Toast.tsx` is `fixed bottom-6`; the card ends at y = 758 of 800, so the 36 px toast (y ≈ 740–776) straddles the card border and the fade mask. Copy also mixes precision: toast says `CG 36.00 m`, strip says `36.0 m`; and "Optimal" is solver jargon.
**Fix:** Anchor the toast inside the aircraft card (`absolute bottom-3 left-1/2` in `AircraftCard.tsx`'s relative wrapper, above the fade) or under the CG strip (`fixed top-[130px]`). Copy: `Optimised · CG 36.0 m · 133 ms`; errors: `Can't place AMA in A2 (AKE only)` and `DL is locked`.

### 16. Empty panel is a 600 px white void with one centred line
**Saw:** After Optimize on Full load: `Unassigned 0 · 0 kg` header and `All ULDs loaded` centred at the top of a 588 px empty column.
**Fix:** `UldPanel.tsx`: when `unassigned.length === 0`, render a load summary instead — `34 ULDs · 106,853 kg`, `Left 53,210 kg / Right 53,643 kg`, `Locked 1`, `Last solve 133 ms · optimal` — in a `dl` with `text-xs text-muted` labels and `tabular` values. That turns the dead space into the "verifiable story" the brief wants. Alternatively `self-start` the panel so it shrinks to its header on desktop.

## P2 — polish

### 17. Gauge label copy and casing
`CG` uppercase 11 px, then `target 36.0 ± 2.0` lowercase with no unit, then `Loaded 106,853 kg · 34/34` capitalised. Fix in `CgStrip.tsx`: `CG 36.0 m · target 36.0 ± 2.0 m · Loaded …` with all three labels in the same 11 px uppercase style, values `tabular`.

### 18. Needle sits outside the track when CG = min
At 7.7 m the 3 px needle is at `left: 0%` translated −50 %, so it hangs 1.5 px past the rounded cap (`CgGauge.tsx`). Clamp `pct` to `[1.5, 98.5]` or make the track `inset-x-1`.

### 19. "Scroll for tail ↓" with a bobbing arrow
`nudgeDown` runs forever at 1.8 s until the user scrolls. The bob is the kind of micro-animation the owner dislikes; the half-cut G row plus the fade mask already say "more below". `AircraftCard.tsx`: keep the caption, drop the animation, or show only the arrow.

### 20. Unequal segmented-control segments and a 432 px control in the header
Segments are 134 / 149 / 139 px because of the `· 34 ULDs` suffixes. Either make them equal (`grid grid-cols-3`, `w-[420px]`) or drop the suffix from the control and show it in the CG strip (`0/34` is already there). `TopBar.tsx`.

### 21. Drawer formulas: default `ui-monospace`, ragged comment column, repo path with no link
`Formula` uses Tailwind `font-mono` (Menlo/SF Mono) inside an IBM Plex page; the comment column drifts on the `W·g = …` line (two characters longer than its neighbours); "See docs/algorithm.md in the repo" is a dead reference for a visitor. `HowItWorksDrawer.tsx`: add `@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono/400.css` and `--font-mono`; pad the left column to a fixed width; link to the GitHub file or remove the sentence.

### 22. Mobile page scrolls 16 px under the fixed bottom strip
`docH = 828` vs viewport 812 because `main` has `pb-20` (80 px) while the strip is 56 px + 8 px gap. `App.tsx`: `pb-[72px]` on mobile (or `pb-16`).

### 23. Mobile gauge caption reads like an axis label
`1,700 kg · 1/34` sits between `NOSE` and `TAIL` directly under the track, so it looks like a tick value on the nose→tail axis. `CgStrip.tsx`: move `loadedShort` to the second row next to the score and leave the gauge with just its two end labels (or the band-edge numbers from finding 2).

### 24. Tile id uses `tracking-wide` + semibold at 11 px in white/80 (4.46 : 1)
Borderline contrast on the id and the letter-spacing makes `ULD-AG` and `AG` look like different fonts. `PositionTile.tsx`: `text-white` (drop the /80) and `tracking-normal`; if you want the id to recede, use size (10 px) not opacity.

### 25. Scrollbar thumb is a 4 px hairline and the fade mask leaves a 8 px notch
Custom thumb is `bg-line-soft` with a 2 px `border-surface` inset → 4 px of #c9ced6. The fade masks stop at `right-2` to avoid it, leaving an unfaded 8 px column at the card's right edge. Either drop the custom thumb border (6 px thumb, `bg-line`) or extend the masks to `right-0` and let them cover the gutter; `AircraftCard.tsx`.
